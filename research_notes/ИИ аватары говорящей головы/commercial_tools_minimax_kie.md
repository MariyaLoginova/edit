## Q1. MiniMax: what exists today, and can it restyle an existing talking-head video (v2v)?

### Takeaway
**Yes, since 31 Jul 2026.** MiniMax H3 ("Hailuo 3.0", API id `MiniMax-H3`) takes video (and audio) as input and does instruction-based editing of uploaded footage. You can swap the character, background or wardrobe, relight, or replace dialogue. Output is 5–15 s per generation at up to 2K, with native stereo audio. Before H3, MiniMax had no v2v: Hailuo 02 and 2.3 were text/image-to-video, and S2V-01 was subject reference from a single image. KIE.ai sells H3 at about 50% of the official price, but its docs only list `minimax-h3/reference-to-video` (no explicit "edit" model id). H3-Base 768p has open weights under a restrictive community license. Speech, Hailuo 02/2.3 and H3's 2K/"Context-IR" modules are API-only.

### Cited Findings
**H3 / Hailuo 3.0, the current flagship (the first MiniMax model that can edit an input video)**
- Unveiled at WAIC on 17 Jul 2026 and released publicly on 31 Jul 2026. It is a "general-purpose multimodal generation model" that outputs video with native stereo sound, up to 15 s, at up to 2K, 24 fps. **[official]** [MiniMax blog](https://www.minimax.io/blog/minimax-h3) via [search summary](https://huggingface.co/blog/ResterChed/minimax-h3-hailuo-3-0); WAIC date from [orcarouter](https://www.orcarouter.ai/blog/minimax-h3-hailuo-3-explained) **[3rd-party]**.
- Text, images, video and audio go into one context. Reported reference limits for the official API: up to 9 images, 3 videos and 3 audio clips, 12 files in total. Each video or audio clip must be 2–15 s, with 15 s total per type. **[3rd-party]** [Atlas Cloud on H3 lip-sync/audio](https://www.atlascloud.ai/blog/tips/minimax-h3-lip-sync-and-audio); [mlq.ai](https://mlq.ai/news/minimax-h3-tops-a-video-editing-arena-but-its-open-weights-exclude-us-users/).
- Edit capabilities as described by partners: character replacement, object swap, relighting, **dialogue replacement**, **background replacement** and VFX, "while keeping unspecified elements intact". **[3rd-party]** [Scenario](https://www.scenario.com/blog/minimax-h3-multimodal-video-generation-scenario). KIE's page lists "subject replacement, motion transfer, background modification, relighting, dialogue changes, voice matching, **wardrobe updates**, product swaps, localized edits". **[KIE]** [kie.ai/minimax-h3](https://kie.ai/minimax-h3).
- WaveSpeed's dedicated H3 **video-edit** endpoint takes a video URL and a prompt, plus up to 9 reference images and 3 reference audio clips. Setting `generate_audio=false` **keeps the input video's original audio**. Duration is 3–15 s and defaults to the input length. Resolution is 480p/540p/768p/1080p. **[3rd-party]** [WaveSpeed blog](https://wavespeed.ai/blog/minimax-h3/minimax-h3-video-edit-extend-540p/). On Runware the source clip goes in `inputs.referenceVideos` and the prompt says what changes and what stays. **[3rd-party]** [Runware H3 editing guide](https://runware.ai/docs/models/minimax-h3/guides/editing-video).
- Official API: model id `MiniMax-H3`, asynchronous V2 task/poll flow, references passed in a content array. I found no official page that documents a dedicated "edit" parameter. **[3rd-party]** [minimax3.org](https://minimax3.org/minimax-h3-api); MiniMax's own video guide lists H3 and H3 Max as the current video models. **[official]** [platform.minimax.io video guide](https://platform.minimax.io/docs/guides/video-generation).
- Quality caveats: one guide says reference-to-video "does not guarantee exact identity … lip sync, timing" ([DomoAI](https://www.domoai.app/blog/minimax-h3-reference-to-video)), and early users report end-of-clip lip-sync or audio drift in complex cases ([search summary of ComfyUI user reports](https://comfyui-wiki.com/en/news/2026-08-03-minimax-h3-open-weights-comfyui)). **[3rd-party]**
- Rankings: #1 in Video Editing, #2 T2V and #3 I2V on Artificial Analysis at its 3 Aug 2026 debut ([mlq.ai](https://mlq.ai/news/minimax-h3-tops-a-video-editing-arena-but-its-open-weights-exclude-us-users/)). It has since been overtaken by Wan 3.0 (see Q6). On the live AA-Video-Editing board, "MiniMax H3 (768p)" has Elo 1119 versus 1187 for Wan 3.0 ([Artificial Analysis](https://artificialanalysis.ai/video/leaderboard/video-editing)).
- **H3 Max**: a post-trained H3 built and served by fal. It is fast (a 5-s clip in under 3 s), outputs 480p/768p, and debuted at #1 in I2V-with-audio on AA. MiniMax's docs call it a joint release ([fal blog](https://blog.fal.ai/introducing-h3-max-by-fal/), [AA on X](https://x.com/ArtificialAnlys/status/2092717615739494424), [platform.minimax.io](https://platform.minimax.io/docs/guides/video-generation)). Morphic disputes that MiniMax "announced" it ([Morphic](https://morphic.com/resources/models/minimax-h3-max)). Picsart sells an "H3 Max Lip Sync" photo+audio→talking video mode (5–14.8 s, length set by the audio) ([Picsart](https://picsart.com/ai-models/minimax-h3-max-lip-sync/)). Runway's "Fall 2026 collection" hosts MiniMax H3 Max inside Runway ([Runway on X](https://x.com/runwayml/status/2100275904366944329)).

- Hailuo 02 (June 2025) was T2V/I2V ([MiniMax news](https://www.minimax.io/news/minimax-hailuo-02)). Hailuo 2.3 official API price: $0.28 (768P, 6 s), $0.56 (768P, 10 s), $0.49 (1080P, 6 s). **MiniMax Speech (TTS and voice cloning, useful for re-voicing an era character)**
- Speech 2.8 official pricing: `speech-2.8-hd` $100 per 1M characters, `speech-2.8-turbo` $60 per 1M characters, on both sync and async endpoints. Async accepts up to 1M characters per request. **[official]** [MiniMax pricing](https://platform.minimax.io/docs/pricing/overview). The family includes Speech 2.8, 2.6 and Voice Design ([invideo](https://invideo.io/blog/minimax-ai-voice-models/)).
- Rapid voice cloning reportedly costs $1.50 per voice, charged on first use (third-party trackers). An older figure of $3 per voice dates from the Speech-02 era. **Not confirmed on the official page.** [Inworld comparison](https://inworld.ai/resources/best-text-to-speech-apis); [minimax-ai.chat](https://minimax-ai.chat/audio/).

**Open weights versus API-only**
- **H3-Base (768p)** weights were published on 3 Aug 2026 at `MiniMaxAI/MiniMax-H3` under the **MiniMax H3 Community License**. Local use is **not permitted in the US, EU, UK or South Korea**. Organisations under $20M revenue may use it commercially for free where permitted. Training other models on its outputs is banned. **H3-Context-IR and H3-Regenerate-2K were not released, so 2K is API-only.** [atoms.dev](https://atoms.dev/blog/minimax-h3-open-weights-multimodal-video-model); [mlq.ai](https://mlq.ai/news/minimax-h3-tops-a-video-editing-arena-but-its-open-weights-exclude-us-users/); [ComfyUI wiki (FL2VA, Ref2VA)](https://comfyui-wiki.com/en/news/2026-08-03-minimax-h3-open-weights-comfyui). Self-hosting is heavy: one report used 2×RTX 5090 and 384 GB RAM and needed 9.3 min for 5 s ([Runpod](https://www.runpod.io/blog/minimax-h3-the-open-weight-omni-modal-video-model-and-what-it-takes-to-run-it), via search summary).
- LLMs: **M1** (June 2025) Apache-2.0; **M2** (Oct 2025) modified-MIT; **M2.5** (Feb 2026) modified-MIT ([HF card](https://huggingface.co/MiniMaxAI/MiniMax-M2.5)); **M2.7** (Mar 2026) weights from April, licence reported as non-commercial (unverified); **M3** (Jun 2026, 428B MoE, about 23B active, 1M context) open weights under a custom MiniMax community licence ([morphllm](https://www.morphllm.com/minimax-m3), [codersera](https://codersera.com/blog/minimax-m3-developer-guide/), [presenc.ai lineage](https://presenc.ai/research/minimax-model-lineage-2026)). M3.1-Flash-Preview (27 Sep 2026) has unknown weight status.
- **API-only**: Hailuo 01/02/2.3, S2V-01, H3 2K and Context-IR, all Speech models (no open speech weights found).

### Inferences
- For "my real talking head → era costume / new location", MiniMax H3 is now a real option. It is also one of the cheapest on KIE (about $0.04/s at 768p, but input seconds are billed too, so a 10-s edit ≈ 20 billed seconds ≈ $0.80 at 768p, or $1.30 at 2K). The 15-s cap means longer monologues must be cut into ≤15-s shots and edited shot by shot. That is a property of video length, not LLM chunking.
- To keep the original performance audio, use an edit mode with audio generation off (as on WaveSpeed). On KIE, test whether `minimax-h3/reference-to-video` preserves the source audio and lip timing, or re-synthesises them.

### Gaps
- No official MiniMax doc page confirmed an explicit "video edit" endpoint or parameter, or the max input length for edit mode. All edit-mode specs come from partners (WaveSpeed, Runware, Scenario).
- The official cloning fee for Speech 2.8 was not confirmed. MiniMax still has no dedicated lip-sync-on-existing-video product or avatar API (none found).
- Whether the H3 Community License allows local use in Russia or other non-listed countries: the sources list only the US, EU, UK and South Korea as excluded. Read the LICENSE file directly.

## Q2. Kling (Kuaishou): O1 → 3.0 Omni edit, Motion Control, Lip-Sync, Avatar, Kling 4.0

### Takeaway
Kling 3.0 Omni ("O3") is the production Kling v2v editor. "Base" reference-video mode edits the uploaded clip, keeps its length and timing, and **keeps the original audio**. Reference video is 3–15 s since the 17 Jun 2026 changelog, and 4K is supported. Motion Control 2.6 and 3.0 drive a **character image** with your filmed motion (up to 30 s). It is a good fit when the "new look" is a reference still of you in costume. Lip-Sync re-syncs existing video to new audio. AI Avatar 2.0 is image+audio only. Kling 4.0 was announced on 28 Sep 2026 with 30-s multi-clip editing, but has **no API yet**. KIE carries O3 transformation, Motion Control 2.6 and 3.0, and Avatar 2.0.

### Cited Findings
- **Kling O1** launched on 1 Dec 2025 as a "unified multimodal" model that merges generation, editing and shot extension in one engine, with 3–10 s generations. Vendor blind tests claim a 230% win ratio vs Runway Aleph on instruction-based transformation (vendor-reported). [Barchart PR](https://www.barchart.com/story/news/36401393/kling-ai-launches-o1-the-industry-s-first-unified-multimodal-video-model-revolutionizing-content-creation-and-editing); [Atlas Cloud](https://www.atlascloud.ai/blog/Kling-Video-O1-Launch-The-World-s-First-Unified-Multimodal-Video-Model). *Superseded by 3.0 Omni.*
- **Kling 3.0 series** (Video 3.0, Video 3.0 Omni, Image 3.0, Image 3.0 Omni) rolled out first to Ultra members ([seenthis.kr](https://seenthis.kr/newspageeng/4684)). The exact release date was not confirmed in the sources.
- Official API changelog: on 23 Apr 2026, `kling-v3-omni` supported everything **except** reference video. On **17 Jun 2026**, reference video was expanded to **3–15 s** and **4K** generation was added. **[official]** [Kling API updates](https://kling.ai/document-api/updates/api).
- Edit semantics: set `video_reference_type=base` and the model modifies the existing video per the prompt. Set it to `feature` and the clip is used only for camera and style ([Replicate kling-v3-omni-video](https://replicate.com/kwaivgi/kling-v3-omni-video)). In base mode the output duration matches the source, and "**audio from the original video is preserved**, but new AI audio cannot be generated in editing mode". Older third-party guides cite 3–10 s and ≤200 MB MP4/MOV ([kling3.pro](https://kling3.pro/blog/kling-3-0-omni-guide), [apimart](https://apimart.ai/blog/kling-3-0-omni-update-4k-video-editing-15s-clips-explained)). With a video input, at most 4 image elements are allowed ([vidmuse](https://vidmuse.ai/blog/kling-3-0-omni)). **[3rd-party]**
- O3 edit pricing varies widely by provider: official Kuaishou endpoint about $0.126–0.168/s plus a $0.05 reference-processing fee ([kling4.co](https://www.kling4.co/blog/kling-3-0-omni-guide-2026)); EvoLink edit from $0.1125/s ([EvoLink](https://evolink.ai/blog/kling-3-o3-api-official-discount-pricing-developers)); APIMart $0.0672/s at 720p. **KIE O3** (generation): 720p 14 credits/s without audio or 18 with; 1080p 18/23 credits/s (≈$0.07–0.115/s), plus a 4K tier. The edit or transformation rate was not shown ([kie.ai/kling-o3](https://kie.ai/kling-o3)). The KIE model id is `kling-3.0-omni/transformation`, and `video_urls` must contain **exactly one** video ([docs.kie.ai via search](https://docs.kie.ai/market/kling/kling-3-0)).
- **Motion Control** (your filmed motion drives a character image): official 1080p costs 1.2 units ≈ $0.168/s ([kling.ai/dev/pricing](https://kling.ai/dev/pricing)). Max 30 s when `character_orientation` follows the video, 10 s when it follows the image ([WaveSpeed](https://wavespeed.ai/models/kwaivgi/kling-v3.0-pro/motion-control)).
  - **KIE 3.0 MC** (`kling-3.0/motion-control`): 720p 20 credits/s ($0.10), 1080p 27 credits/s ($0.135) ([kie.ai/kling-3-motion-control](https://kie.ai/kling-3-motion-control)).
  - **KIE 2.6 MC**: 720p 11 credits/s ($0.055), 1080p 18 credits/s ($0.09). Motion video must be 3–30 s, MP4/MOV, ≤100 MB. Image JPEG/PNG ≤10 MB. Prompt ≤2,500 characters ([kie.ai/kling-2.6-motion-control](https://kie.ai/kling-2.6-motion-control), [docs.kie.ai MC](https://docs.kie.ai/market/kling/motion-control), [MC v3](https://docs.kie.ai/market/kling/motion-control-v3)).
  - KIE says 3.0 MC "preserves facial identity" of the character image **[KIE]** ([kie.ai/market/video-editing](https://kie.ai/market/video-editing)).
- **Lip-Sync** (existing video + new audio): resellers list $0.06 per 5 s and $0.11 per 10 s ([useapi.net](https://useapi.net/docs/api-kling-v1)), $0.0168/s ([FairStack](https://fairstack.ai/models/kling-lipsync-a2v)), and $0.15/run up to $1.80 per 60 s ([WaveSpeed](https://wavespeed.ai/models/kwaivgi/kling-lipsync/audio-to-video)). The sources disagree on whether the input is video or image. No Kling Lip-Sync endpoint was found on KIE.
- **AI Avatar 2.0** takes image + audio (not video). Official app pricing is 4 credits/s Std and 8 credits/s Pro, with a 2-s minimum ([therundown](https://www.therundown.ai/tools/kling-avatar-2-0)). fal charges $0.0562/s Std and $0.115/s Pro ([fal Std](https://fal.ai/models/fal-ai/kling-video/ai-avatar/v2/standard), [fal Pro](https://fal.ai/models/fal-ai/kling-video/ai-avatar/v2/pro)). **KIE**: 720p 8 credits/s ($0.04), 1080p 16 credits/s ($0.08), up to 15 s per generation on KIE, although Kling markets up to 5-min continuous generation ([kie.ai/kling-ai-avatar](https://kie.ai/kling-ai-avatar)).
- **Kling 4.0**: announced on 28 Sep 2026. 4.0 Flash has been in early access since then, with a full launch "in October". **API not released.** Editing accepts up to 5 clips (1 primary), each 3–30 s, 30 s combined, and can change camera angle, style, background, scene and body movement while keeping the rest stable. Output is up to 30 s and 4K ([WaveSpeed](https://wavespeed.ai/blog/ai-news/kling-4-0-release-date/), [fal learn](https://fal.ai/learn/tools/what-is-kling-4-0), [Runware "coming soon"](https://runware.ai/kling-4)). KIE already has a Kling 4.0 landing page ([kie.ai/kling-4-0](https://kie.ai/kling-4-0)); availability is unverified.

### Inferences
- Two Kling paths fit the trend: (a) **O3 base edit** for "same clip, new costume or background, same audio", ≤15 s; (b) **Motion Control**: make a still of yourself in costume (any image model), then drive it with your filmed performance for up to 30 s. In (b) the voice is not regenerated, but mouth shapes come from motion transfer, so plan a lip-sync post-pass if lips look off.

## Q3. Runway: Aleph 2.0 (v2v editing) and Act-Two (performance capture)

### Takeaway
Runway **Aleph 2.0** (21 May 2026) is the strongest Western "edit my real footage" model. It makes targeted prompt edits (background, lighting, style, objects) on clips **up to 30 s at 1080p** for **$0.28/s** via the official API. The original **Gen-4 Aleph was retired on 30 Jul 2026**. **Act-Two** costs $0.05/s and maps your filmed performance onto a character image or video. KIE still documents an Aleph endpoint, but its price and model version were not confirmed.

### Cited Findings
- Aleph 2.0 and Edit Studio launched on **21 May 2026**. They are available on all paid plans in the web app. Edits run up to 30 s at 1080p, and the model "chang[es] only what you want … keeping everything else just as it was" ([Runway news](https://runway.com/news/introducing-aleph-2-and-edit-studio), [aipedia](https://www.aipedia.wiki/news/2026-05-21-runway-aleph-20-edit-studio/), [GIGAZINE](https://gigazine.net/gsc_news/en/20260525-runway-aleph-video-edit-ai)).
- Edit types mentioned are background, lighting, style and objects (example: overcast → golden hour). No source specifically demonstrated **outfit swaps on talking heads**. One guide notes wardrobe consistency across edits ([Picsart blog](https://picsart.com/blog/runway-aleph-2-0-launch/), [deep-nostalgia guide](https://deep-nostalgia-ai.com/hub/how-to-use-aleph-2)).
- API pricing: `aleph2` costs 28 credits/s with a 56-credit minimum, at $0.01 per credit, so **$0.28/s** (≈$1.40 per 5 s). Input is ≤30 s, ≤1080p, ≤30 fps. ProRes and HDR add 5–40 credits/s. API credits are separate from app plans ([Runway API pricing](https://docs.dev.runwayml.com/guides/pricing/), [Atlas](https://www.atlascloud.ai/blog/tips/runway-aleph), [OpenRouter](https://openrouter.ai/runway/aleph-2)). An older figure of 15 credits/s refers to the original Aleph. *Superseded.*
- **Gen-4 Aleph retired 30 Jul 2026**; migrate to `aleph2` ([Runway API changelog](https://docs.dev.runwayml.com/api-details/api_changelog/)).
- **Act-Two**: 5 credits/s = **$0.05/s** (official). Resellers charge $0.04–0.065/s. It combines a driving performance video with a character reference. Quality degrades with occlusion, unusual camera motion and mismatched anatomy. **No max duration was found** ([Runway API pricing](https://docs.dev.runwayml.com/guides/pricing/), [aimlapi](https://aimlapi.com/models/runway-act-two), [CometAPI](https://www.cometapi.com/models/runway/act-two/)). No Act-Two successor was found as of Oct 2026.
- Other recent Runway releases: Gen-4.5 (T2V/I2V), Runway Ruby (SDR→HDR grading), GWM Worlds 2 (3 Sep 2026), and third-party models in-app (MiniMax H3 Max, "Flux Video upscaler and edit", Seedance 2.5) ([Runway changelog](https://runway.com/changelog), [Releasebot](https://releasebot.io/updates/runwayai), [Runway on X](https://x.com/runwayml/status/2100275904366944329)).
- KIE: Aleph via `POST /api/v1/aleph/generate` with a prompt, a reference video URL and an optional callback or watermark ([docs.kie.ai Aleph](https://docs.kie.ai/runway-api/generate-aleph-video); a newer doc path is [runway-aleph-api](https://docs.kie.ai/runway-aleph-api/generate-aleph-video)). KIE's general Runway page lists Gen-4 Turbo at 12 credits (720p) and 30 credits (1080p) per video ([kie.ai/runway-api](https://kie.ai/runway-api)). No KIE Aleph price was found, and it is **unconfirmed whether KIE routes to Aleph 2.0** after the Gen-4 Aleph retirement.

### Inferences
- Aleph 2.0 is the best documented fit for "same shot, new set/lighting/style, same performance" at up to 30 s. At $0.28/s it is about 3–7× the per-second cost of H3 or Wan on KIE. Act-Two is the cheapest way to put your performance onto a stylised character, but the identity becomes the character's, not yours.

### Gaps

## Q4. Luma: Ray3 Modify → Ray 3.2 Modify Video

### Takeaway
Luma's Modify Video (now running on **Ray 3.2**) is purpose-built to "replace a wall, world or wardrobe while preserving lighting and the acting performance". The output keeps the source length, ≤18–20 s at 1080p. A strength preset with nine levels controls fidelity. It is priced per clip (about $0.72–2.16 per 5 s) and is **not on KIE**.

### Cited Findings
- The product calls it "Video To Video" and the API calls it Modify Video. Ray 3.2 is behind every `video_edit` request. A **strength preset with 9 values in 3 bands** matters more than the prompt ([Atlas Cloud Luma guide](https://www.atlascloud.ai/blog/tips/luma-modify-video)).
- Marketing: output up to 20 s at 1080p with the same duration as the source. The developer docs reportedly allow a source of ≤200 MB and **≤18 s**. Another review gives 20 s at 24 fps, 15 s at 30 fps and 7 s at 60 fps. The 2025 Modify Video API changelog said ≤10 s and 100 MB (*superseded*) ([lumalabs.ai/ray](https://lumalabs.ai/ray), [therundown Ray 3.2](https://www.therundown.ai/tools/ray-3-2), [Luma changelog](https://lumalabs.ai/changelog/modify-video-api-release)).

## Q7. Lip-sync and avatar post-pass services (only where "keep my real performance, change my look" applies)

### Takeaway
After restyling, a **video-in lip-sync pass** repairs mouth drift. **Sync (sync.so)** is the reference option: sync-3 is 4K and occlusion-aware at about $0.11–0.13/s; lipsync-2-pro costs about $0.067–0.083/s. Kling Lip-Sync is a cheaper alternative. HeyGen, Hedra, Captions/Mirage and Arcads mostly generate a **new** performance from image/twin + audio, so they do not keep your filmed performance. **Higgsfield Genjutsu Object Swap** (1 Sep 2026) is the closest consumer tool for "swap only my outfit/character, keep the shot", but no public API was found.

### Cited Findings
- **Sync**: sync-3 is the default and most powerful model. It works on close-ups, extreme angles and obstructions, outputs **4K natively**, and processes the whole shot rather than chunks ([sync.so sync-3](https://sync.so/docs/models/sync-3), [lipsync models](https://sync.so/docs/models/lipsync), [Morphic](https://morphic.com/resources/models/sync-v3)). WaveSpeed reports a 16B-parameter model, 95+ languages and 5 sync modes for length mismatch (third-party) ([WaveSpeed](https://wavespeed.ai/blog/posts/introducing-sync-lipsync-3-on-wavespeedai/)). Price at 25 fps: sync-3 about $0.107–0.133/s, lipsync-2-pro about $0.067–0.083/s, lipsync-2 from about $0.025/s. Plans start at $5 and $19 per month ([sync.so pricing](https://sync.so/pricing), [billing](https://sync.so/docs/product/billing)). fal offers lipsync-2-pro at $5/min ([fal](https://fal.ai/models/fal-ai/sync-lipsync/v2)). Video length is up to 30 min depending on plan; the free tier allows sync-3 at 15 s ([intro](https://sync.so/docs/introduction), [free trial](https://sync.so/docs/free-trial)). **Not found on KIE.**
- **Kling Lip-Sync**: see Q2 (resellers $0.0168/s to $0.15/run).
- **Higgsfield**: Recast (around Nov 2025) does full character replacement while preserving gestures and camera ([X post](https://x.com/rezkhere/status/1989040232168329226)). **Genjutsu** (launched 1 Sep 2026) offers *Motion Transfer* (keeps camera and timing, rebuilds the rest) and *Object Swap* (replaces one selected element, such as a character, **outfit** or location, and keeps the rest). Clips are 3–30 s with up to 40 reference images. Users report long render queues; the Plus plan is about €59/month ([bottlerocket](https://www.bottlerocketcontent.com/higgsfield-genjutsu-motion-transfer/), [kavel](https://www.kavel.ai/blog/higgsfield-genjutsu-price-guide), [chatgate](https://chatgate.ai/post/higgsfield-genjutsu)). **[3rd-party]**

The twin-creation API is Enterprise-only ([HeyGen help](https://help.heygen.com/en/articles/10060327-heygen-api-pricing-explained), [realtimeavatar](https://realtimeavatar.ai/blog/heygen-api-pricing-explained), [WaveSpeed](https://wavespeed.ai/models/heygen/avatar-v/digital-twin)).

- **Captions / Mirage** is a consumer app with a chat-based editor for uploaded footage. Mirage Edit (Apr 2025) generated non-existent actors. **No public API was found.** Credit tiers are $9.99–69.99/month ([Captions blog](https://captions.ai/blog/editing-videos-is-now-as-easy-as-typing), [Mirage on X](https://x.com/getcaptionsapp/status/1912534547519975844), [prizmad](https://prizmad.com/review/captions-ai)).
